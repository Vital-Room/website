FAQ

What does the session actually involve?

A 1 hour interactive and engaging wellbeing session around a physical or mental health topic. Filled with the latest science, stories from my near decade long medical experience and impactful exercises. There may even be a spoken word poetry piece thrown in there, which as you can see from the feedback above, is very well received!

A particular favourite is the Q&A after the session, questions are submitted anonymously during the talk which I then answer after the session. It's nice to have access to a Lifestyle Medicine and Preventative Health GP where you can ask those nagging questions about a topic.

What resources are included?

Pre-event materials including a branded promotional flyer and email template to send out to your team about the event. After the event, you get an Impact Report which demonstrates whether people enjoyed it and whether they improved their knowledge.

You will also receive a PDF 1 page guide summary of the topic that you can add to your workplace wellbeing page. You are permitted to record the session for those that are unable to attend.

For an in-person session, what do you require?

Just a room and an audience! If needed, I will bring a PAT tested speaker and microphone however if you have a communications team with an in-built set up that's always convenient.

Does The Vital Room have appropriate insurance?

Yes, professional indemnity cover, public liability and cyber security insurance are all in place, up to date and available upon request.

How do I book?

It's always nice to have a quick 15 minute chat about your event or what you were hoping for. You can book in here. That being said, I know you're busy so feel free to send me an email at hello@thevitalroom.com with a rough idea of what you'd like and location. I aim to respond within 6 hours.
